# LLM Text Evaluation Framework (QTRC)

## Overview
QTRC (Qur’anic Tafsir Research Canvas) is a structured system designed to organize, assess, and evaluate the epistemological coherence of Qur’anic tafsir research.

Its primary function is not to generate content, but to structure and validate research design, including the use of AI-assisted workflows.

---

## Core Function

QTRC operates as:

- A framework for structuring tafsir research  
- A system for evaluating coherence between research components  
- A method for identifying epistemic gaps and inconsistencies  
- A guide for research progression (Basic → Intermediate → Expert)  

---

## Scope

QTRC is designed specifically for:

- Interpretative and methodological Qur’anic tafsir research  

It is not intended for:
- Content generation  
- Preaching or da‘wah  
- General-purpose AI assistance  
- Empirical “Living Qur’an” studies  

---

## Problem

In AI-assisted research and writing, several recurring issues are observed:

- Hallucinations (fabricated or unsupported information)  
- Contextual errors  
- Distorted or unclear meaning  

These issues require structured evaluation rather than direct content generation.

---

## Approach

QTRC provides a structured system that:

- Organizes research components into interconnected blocks  
- Evaluates consistency between research elements  
- Avoids unsupported assumptions  
- Maintains strict disciplinary boundaries  

---

## Structure

QTRC consists of 11 interconnected components:

1. Topic  
2. Research Question  
3. Objective  
4. Corpus Qur’aniyyah  
5. Context  
6. Data & Sources  
7. Theoretical Framework  
8. Method of Tafsir  
9. Analysis Strategy  
10. Contribution & Novelty  
11. Title  

Each component is evaluated in relation to others and cannot be finalized independently.

---

## Evaluation Logic

The system follows a structured process:

1. Identify the relevant component  
2. Check dependencies between components  
3. Detect ambiguity or inconsistency  
4. Evaluate strictly based on provided information  
5. Pause when information is insufficient  
6. Assign epistemic status  

---

## Evaluation Criteria

Each component is assessed based on:

- Coherence  
- Consistency  
- Clarity  
- Alignment with research objectives  
- Epistemic validity  

---

## Example (Simplified)

### Issue:
Misalignment between research question and method  

### Evaluation:
- Coherence: Low  
- Dependency: Not aligned  
- Status: Not Valid  

### Requirement:
Clarify methodological alignment with the research question  

---

## Project Scope

QTRC is a conceptual and experimental system for:

- Structuring research design  
- Evaluating epistemic consistency  
- Supporting AI-assisted analysis (without delegating interpretation to AI)  

---

## Note

QTRC does not generate tafsir content.  
It functions as a structured system for organizing and evaluating research methodology.
